The problem

Right now someone is searching for what you sell — and finding your competitor.

Word of mouth still ends up on a phone. Someone recommends you, the next person searches your name — and what they find there decides whether they message you or keep scrolling.

Questions

What everybody asks.

Do I need a domain? What does it cost?

Yes, and you want your own — yourbusiness.com looks serious and is yours permanently. It runs $12–20 USD a year, paid directly to the registrar and registered in your name, not mine. I'll walk you through buying it in about ten minutes, and I make nothing on it.

Why so cheap? What's the catch?

There's no office, no salespeople, no three-hour discovery meeting. I write the code by hand, so I'm not paying for licenses and subscriptions that I'd have to bill back to you. And I work from Chihuahua, not from a city with agency rent. The price is low because my costs are low, not because the work is less.

What happens if I stop paying the $29?

You tell me and that's it, no penalty. I hand over every file, and the domain is already in your name, so you can move it to another host or give it to someone else. I don't leave you empty-handed or hold the site hostage.

I don't have good photos or a logo.

That's the most normal thing in the world. We work with what's on your phone — a lot can be done with cropping, correction and good placement. If there's really nothing, I tell you exactly which photos to take and how: your own phone, daylight, five minutes. And if you have no logo, I'll make you a simple clean wordmark at no extra cost.

Can I change things myself?

Small changes you send me on WhatsApp and they're included in the $29: hours, prices, phone, new photos, a paragraph. Usually same day. That's deliberate — it means the site can't break because something got moved by accident, which is what actually happens with editing dashboards. If you'd rather edit it yourself, that can be set up too; just tell me.

How many pages, and how much can be revised?

Up to 5 pages within the $199 — usually home, services, work or gallery, about, and contact. Revisions are included until you're happy: colors, wording, section order, photos. What gets quoted separately is starting over with a completely different design, or adding new pages later on.

Do you do online stores?

Simple ones, yes, quoted separately. A catalog with a cart that sends the order through WhatsApp works very well for local businesses — that's how the Carnes Hildebrandt site works, go open it. What I don't do is large stores with live inventory and card payments; for that I'll tell you honestly what to use instead.

What if I don't like how it turns out?

You pay half at the start and half when it goes live, precisely for that reason: you don't pay the rest until you've seen it working and you're satisfied. And before I write a line of code I show you the design direction, so nobody gets a surprise at the end.

Do you work outside Chihuahua?

Yes. All of it works over WhatsApp and the work is the same wherever you are. The only difference is that locally I can come see the business and take the photos myself, which always helps.
